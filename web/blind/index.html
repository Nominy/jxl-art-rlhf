Blind A/B: JPEG XL art before and after RLHF

Each round shows two images. Each is a JPEG XL tree program of at most 256 bytes, written by a 3M-parameter model running in your browser. One comes from the model before RLHF, the other from the same model after 820 RLHF steps on one person's picks. Pick the one you like; after 10 rounds you see which was which.
